Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026 · Updated November 13, 2026

In short: Adhan Times calculates prayer times on your device. The app does not collect your personal information, does not send it to our servers, and does not share or sell it.

This policy explains how the Adhan Times app for iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and Android (“the app”), published by Conelab IT Consultants Inc. (“we”, “us”), handles information.

Information the app uses

  • Location. With your permission, the app uses your device’s location while you are using it to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. The app never uses your location in the background or while it is closed. Your location is not sent to us. To show the name of your city, the app uses your device’s built-in place lookup service, provided by Apple on Apple devices and Google on Android, under their privacy policies. If you decline location access, the app uses a default city that you can change in Settings.
  • Settings. Your preferences, such as calculation method, Asr method, reminder choices and appearance, are stored on your device. On Apple devices they may be shared between the app, its widgets and its Apple Watch app on your own devices.
  • Notifications. Prayer reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. The app does not use a server to send you notifications.

What we do not do

  • We do not require an account or ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • We do not collect analytics, usage data or crash reports through the app.
  • We do not show ads, use advertising identifiers or track you across apps and websites.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your information with third parties.

Permissions

The app asks for location access to calculate prayer times and for notification access to deliver prayer reminders. You can change either permission at any time in your device’s settings. The app keeps working without them, with a default location and no reminders.

Children

Adhan Times is suitable for all ages. Because the app does not collect personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children.

Data retention and deletion

Everything the app stores stays on your device. Deleting the app removes its settings from that device.

This website

The prayer times on adhantimes.conelabs.org estimate your city from your IP address using GeoJS, a third-party lookup service, under its own privacy policy. If you choose “Use my precise location”, your browser asks for permission and the location is used only in your browser. Your settings and location are saved in your browser’s local storage on your device. We don’t receive them, and we don’t use cookies, analytics or advertising on the website.

App stores

When you download the app, Apple or Google may collect information under their own privacy policies. We do not receive personal information about you from them through the app.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and its effective date.
